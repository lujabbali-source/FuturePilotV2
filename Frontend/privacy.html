Privacy Policy

This page is a placeholder. FuturePilot is under active development and this policy has not yet been finalized or reviewed by legal counsel. Do not treat this as a complete or binding privacy notice.

What we store

Your email, name (if provided), and a securely hashed version of your password - never the password itself. Your assessment answers and the results generated from them, tied to your account once you register or log in.

How we protect it

Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 before they ever touch storage. Sessions use bearer tokens, not your raw credentials, and expire automatically.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with third parties beyond what's needed to run the platform itself.

Questions

Reach out at soporte@futurepilot.com with any questions about your data.
